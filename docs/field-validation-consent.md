# Consent for pitch review (template)

Read this to the participant, or send it, before they share a pitch. Keep their written yes (an email reply is enough) outside this repository.

**What this is.** A test of an early diagnostic tool, OfferRoast, that reads a cold-outreach pitch and points to a likely weakness. It does not predict replies or results.

**What happens to your pitch.**
- The current test version runs rules in the browser. Your text is not sent to an AI provider or stored on a server.
- I will note your answers to a few questions. I will not record your name, company, or contact details with them.
- By default, I delete the pitch text after this session. I keep only the de-identified answers.

**Optional, separate yes/no.** May I keep a de-identified copy of your pitch (names, domains, and contact details removed) with my notes, to test future versions of the tool? You can say no and still take part. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

**Other points.**
- Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
- Nothing here is a guarantee of results.
- If a payment or pre-order is offered later, it is a separate, explicit choice. No payment is taken during this session.
